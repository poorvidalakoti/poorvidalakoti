My Story

From a small town to the world stage.

I grew up in Uttarakhand, India — a small town tucked into the Himalayas — where sport wasn't just a hobby, it was everything. Cricket, basketball, baseball. I represented my university, captained teams, and aimed to play professionally.

Then in 2022, an injury forced me to stop. Not to give up — to pivot. If I couldn't be on the field as a player, I'd shape sport from the outside. I did two masters degrees. Then I made the biggest leap of my life.

I moved continents. No family nearby. No familiar faces. A financial loan, a new culture, a new climate — and a relentless drive to prove that the leap was worth it. Some days were remarkable. Some were brutal. I kept going.

While studying my MSc at Loughborough, I worked, volunteered at major events, contributed to EDI policy, managed operations, taught students, and built a career — all at the same time. This wasn't accidental. It was the plan.
